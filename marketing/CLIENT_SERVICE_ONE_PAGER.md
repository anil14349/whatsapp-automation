## What you are buying

A **hosted WhatsApp appointment service** on your clinic’s WhatsApp number.

Patients can book, view, cancel, reschedule, and receive reminders.  
Doctors can view schedules and manage availability from WhatsApp.  
You see all appointments in a **Google Sheet we share with you**.

This is a **managed service** (like website hosting), not a one-time software purchase.

---

## Ownership — who has what

| Item | Owner | Notes |
|------|-------|-------|
| Clinic WhatsApp phone number | **Clinic** | Patients always message your number |
| Patient names, phones, appointments | **Clinic** (data controller) | Visible in shared sheet |
| Google Sheet (appointment register) | **Provider hosts** · **Clinic has shared access** | Editor or Viewer as agreed |
| Doctors’ Google Calendars | **Clinic / doctors** | We need edit access to create/update events |
| Meta WhatsApp app & webhook | **Provider** | Required to run the service |
| Booking software (Apps Script) | **Provider** | Licensed for use while subscribed; not sold unless separate buyout |
| Message templates & flow text | **Provider** | Customizable within agreed scope |

**Plain English:** Your patients and appointment data are yours. The engine that powers WhatsApp booking is ours to operate and maintain while you subscribe.

- We process appointment data **only to run the service** for your clinic.  
- We do not sell patient data to third parties.  
- Sheet access limited to provider ops + clinic contacts you designate.  
- Minimize sensitive medical data in WhatsApp messages (names, slots, phone — not clinical notes).  
- Clinic is responsible for patient consent as required by local practice.
